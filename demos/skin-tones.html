Skin tones

Every emoji with skin-tone variants comes in the default yellow plus the five Fitzpatrick skin-tone modifiers (U+1F3FB to U+1F3FF). Mixed-tone couples and families use one modifier per person. Pick a tone to restyle the sample text, or browse every base emoji below.
