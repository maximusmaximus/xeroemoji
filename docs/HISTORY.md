# History and sources

Xero Emoji is Max Infeld's emoji set in the Xerographer style. Max dates the start of the project to **2016**. This page collects
the public record found online, with links. Everything here is quoted from or linked to the original posts; nothing is reconstructed.
Research done 2026-10-18 (PT) via the public Steem API (`api.steemit.com`) and the Steemit, DTube and web pages linked below.

## The source link
The link Max gave, `https://share.google/aaHK2gUcPHNJfSWny`, redirects to the Steemit profile
**[@sandwich](https://steemit.com/@sandwich)** ("Sandwich", "Cat Herder, Documentarian & EOS Distribution Expert", Budapest).
That blog feed lists two Xeromoji posts by **@max-infeld**. Those two posts are the primary sources below.
The emoji posts themselves are signed by Max and published from his account, **[@max-infeld](https://steemit.com/@max-infeld)** (on Steemit since 11 May 2016).

## 1. "Happy World Emoji Day! Preview our NEW Emoji set only on STEEMIT!" (World Emoji Day 2017)
- Link: <https://steemit.com/emoji/@max-infeld/happy-world-emoji-day-preview-our-new-emoji-set-only-on-steemit>
- Author: `@max-infeld`; signed "-Max Infeld", "Xerographer Fonts | Xeromoji".
- Posted: 2017-07-18 04:10 UTC = **Monday 17 July 2017, 9:10 PM PDT**, which is World Emoji Day. Category `#emoji`; tags emoji, steemit, fonts, graphicdesign, licensing.
- What it says:
  - The set was announced under its early name **Xeromoji**: "[Xerographer Fonts] is announcing: [Xeromoji] -- a NEW full [UNICODE Emoji Font]!"
  - It was a preview only: "This is just a special preview... so you can't download and use it in your apps or designs...yet..but we're working around the clock to finish this ASAP."
  - Planned format: "We're expected to launch this emoji set as [SVGinOT](https://github.com/eosrei/emojione-color-font) by the end of the year." (SVGinOT means SVG-in-OpenType color font.)
  - Where it was made: "BTW we're designing and programming Xeromoji with 100% love in California."
  - It asked Steemit for feedback, and for ideas on licensing fonts and emoji with cryptocurrencies or tokens.
- Images: a "Happy World Emoji Day! Special preview only on Steemit" banner, plus 17 preview glyphs, each given as name, codepoint and how "Your Browser Displays" it:
  - smiling face with heart eyes (written "U+F60D" in the post), zipper-mouth face, loudly crying face, face vomiting, nerd face and face with monocle;
  - skull and crossbones, and pile of poo ("Our favorite glyph!");
  - man office worker, man scientist: dark skin tone, woman astronaut, Santa Claus: dark skin tone, man mage, man getting haircut: light skin tone and merman;
  - man and woman zombie, and man facepalming: medium-light skin tone.
  - The images are hosted on steemitimages.com, e.g. the [banner](https://steemitimages.com/DQmRiYWczD1s2hs8wbuhMvisjrpvLrcWG8mspf1CH4y2Xdf/header%20for%20steemit-01.jpg) and the [heart-eyes face](https://steemitimages.com/DQmXTzfc6ng7WdCR287Qc86acETGkYpLiPKq92thq7Wm81Z/xerographer%20fonts%20emoji%20demos%20copyright%202017-02.jpg). The file names read "xerographer fonts emoji demos copyright 2017".
  - These glyphs show the same house style as this set: a thick black outline, a glossy yellow gradient face, big white eyes, and an open mouth with a red tongue.
- The banner font is credited in the post: "The font on the banner is [Perceptual](http://www.dafont.com/perceptual.font), also by Xerographer Fonts!"
- Replies on the day included "Wow! You've been working a lot! Great post." (@michaelstobiersk) and "I called them Steemojis." (@flexifriday).

## Elsewhere on the web
- [Xerographer Fonts on dafont.com](https://www.dafont.com/max-infeld.d3584): the foundry profile, under the name Max Infeld.
- [xerographer.github.io](https://xerographer.github.io/): "Open Source Creative Commons Licensed SVG-in-OpenType Polychromatic Color Fonts". These are earlier color fonts (Gradiently, Multicoloure, Reinebow, Freizer) in the same SVG-in-OT format that Xeromoji was planned for.
- [Fyrd/caniuse#2555](https://github.com/Fyrd/caniuse/issues/2555) (2016) uses xerographer.github.io/reinebow as its example SVG-in-OT color font.
- The searches found no download or release of the 2017 Xeromoji font. This repository is the first full public release of the set.

## From Xeromoji to Xero Emoji
| | Xeromoji preview (2017) | Xero Emoji (this repo, 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| scope | "a NEW full UNICODE Emoji Font", 17 preview glyphs | all 3,963 fully-qualified emoji of Unicode Emoji 18.0 |
| format | planned as an SVGinOT color font | SVG + 512 px PNG per emoji, plus a COLRv1 color font (`fonts/`) |
| skin tones | some, e.g. dark-tone scientist and Santa | every emoji that supports them, in a defined 5-tone palette |
| quality | n/a | every file scored by `qc/xeroqc` against the house requirements |
